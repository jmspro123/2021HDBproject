Project Objective

The goal of this project was to predict HDB resale prices and identify the key drivers behind price variation in Singapore’s housing market. The analysis combines exploratory data analysis, unsupervised learning, and supervised predictive modeling to assess how features such as floor area, remaining lease, distance to CBD, and amenity access affect resale values.

R Programming Predictive Modeling Statistical Analysis Housing Analytics

Why This Matters

Housing is one of the most important financial decisions in Singapore. Understanding how resale prices respond to location, size, and property features helps evaluate market behavior, assess value signals, and support informed decisions for buyers, sellers, and analysts.

Methodology

Exploratory Analysis Reviewed distributions, summary statistics, and relationships between resale prices and housing attributes.
Unsupervised Learning K-means clustering, PCA, and hierarchical clustering were used to discover structure in the data.
Regression Modeling Multiple linear regression, polynomial regression, and KNN regression were used for price prediction.
Classification Models Logistic regression and decision tree models were used to classify whether a unit is above the median price threshold.

Key Findings

  • ✓Resale price is strongly associated with property size, distance to the CBD, and remaining lease duration.
  • ✓Feature-rich regression models achieved very strong explanatory power, with an adjusted R² of 0.9266.
  • ✓Logistic regression produced a high classification performance with AUC = 0.94, highlighting practical predictive signal.
  • ✓Model comparison showed that no single method is sufficient; different algorithms provide complementary insights.
